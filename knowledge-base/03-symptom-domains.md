# Symptom Domains and Correlation Synthesis

The QEEG.com.au platform quantifies clinical symptoms across eleven standardised domains captured on the intake checklist and correlated against quantitative EEG and TOVA continuous-performance data.

## Domain List

1. Inattention — sustained focus deficit, distractibility, carelessness.
2. Hyperactivity / Impulsivity — motor restlessness, interrupted-verbal behaviour.
3. Anxiety — generalised worry, autonomic hyperarousal, somatic tension.
4. Mood / Depression — persistent low mood, anhedonia, withdrawal.
5. Rumination — repetitive negative thinking, cognitive hyper-fixation.
6. Emotional Regulation — affective volatility, low frustration tolerance.
7. Sleep Difficulties — onset latency, nocturnal waking, non-restorative sleep.
8. Executive Function / Organisation — working memory, cognitive inflexibility.
9. Oppositional / Behavioural Difficulties — defiance, impulse-control deficit.
10. Social Difficulties — pragmatic language friction, social withdrawal.
11. Sensory Processing — auditory and visual hypersensitivity, sensory overload.

## Likert Scale

Each domain uses a forced 0-4 Likert rating: 0 Absent, 1 Mild, 2 Moderate, 3 Marked, 4 Severe or Pervasive. No blank domains are accepted.

## Synthesis Disclaimers

All generated reports carry two mandatory disclosures: a neurofeedback conflict-of-interest declaration, and an AI-assisted-generation disclaimer. Correlation findings are clinical decision-support outputs; they are not a substitute for independent clinical judgement.
